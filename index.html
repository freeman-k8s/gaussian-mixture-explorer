Gaussian Mixture Explorer

A two-component Gaussian mixture: a weighted sum of two normal densities.

Probability density function
f(t)= x·N(t;μ1,σ12) + y·N(t;μ2,σ22) ,y=1−x
f(t)= xσ12π exp⁡ (−(t−μ1)22σ12) + yσ22π exp⁡ (−(t−μ2)22σ22)

N(t; μ, σ²) is the normal density with mean μ and variance σ². The weight x belongs to component 1 (red), y = 1 − x to component 2 (blue).

Convention: N(μ, σ) denotes a normal distribution with mean μ and standard deviation σ. The weights are constrained to x ∈ [0, 1], y = 1 − x so that f integrates to one. Note that this is a mixture of densities; it differs from the distribution of the linear combination xA + yB of independent normal variables, which would itself be normal.
